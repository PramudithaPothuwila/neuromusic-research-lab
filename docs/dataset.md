## Filename convention (provisional)

`<source>_<label>_<participant>_<song>[_from_<SRCnnn>][_T<nn>].csv`, e.g.
`SRC006_SOURCE_P004_Nadagam_Geeya.csv`. The meaning of `SOURCE` is not assumed. Song tokens
are converted to a canonical key (lower-case alphanumerics), so `Hadisida_Manda`,
`Hadisida Manda` and `HadisidaManda` all become `hadisidamanda`. The regex and aliases are
configurable in `configs/dataset.yaml`; metadata can be corrected in the dashboard.

## Provenance audit (2026-10-29 import, 87 files)

Filenames do not guarantee independent recordings. Every file is fingerprinted (SHA-256) and
summarised by a signal signature (z-scored first differences of the first 4096 samples of AF3,
O1, T8). Signatures correlating at r >= 0.9 form a *signal family*.

| Provenance | Files | Treatment |
|---|---|---|
| original | 31 | analysable (1 failed validation: SRC004 is 3.9 s long) |
| derived_copy | 28 | excluded: near-identical signal of an earlier file, relabelled as another participant |
| identical_copy / label_conflict (byte-identical) | 2 | excluded |
| label_conflict (near-duplicate) | 11 | excluded: one signal carries different song labels, so the stimulus is unknown |
| synthetic (`SYNTH*`) | 15 | excluded: synthetic files derived from real recordings |

Examples: SRC006 (P004, Nadagam Geeya) is byte-identical to SRC028 (P020, Sandaganawa) and
SRC065 (P051, Matath Kaviyak Liyanna); SRC018 (P012, Nubata Dunnu Ale) matches SRC067 (P053,
Sobana); most files SRC038–SRC074 duplicate files SRC001–SRC035.

Result: **30 independent analysable recordings, 22 participants, 16 songs**; most participants
heard one song. The full per-file table is regenerated by `neuromusic scan` at
`data/metadata/recording_manifest.csv` (columns: provenance, signal_family, duplicate_of_file,
exclusion_reason, clean_name, song_title). Exclusions are recorded on each recording and can be
overridden manually; manual decisions are never overwritten by a re-audit.

## Song metadata

* `data/metadata/song_attributes.csv` – 20 songs, verbatim from the owner (original header
  spellings kept; mapped to normalised names on load). `*_Proxy`, tempo, energy, brightness and
  flatness appear audio-derived; `*_Survay` appear to be song-level survey means. Scales,
  tools and respondents are undocumented. These are song properties, **not** per-participant
  ratings, and are only used for exploratory song-level associations (n = songs).
* `data/metadata/song_sources.json` – reference videos for 21 titles (`Mai Mal` has no EEG).
  The owner confirmed on 2026-10-29 that the supplied audio files are the exact files played
  during acquisition; `recording_identity_verified` is true with that statement as
  `identity_evidence` (recorded by `neuromusic stimuli confirm`).

## Stimulus audio

`data/stimuli/audio/<song_key>.<ext>` with a `<song_key>.json` provenance sidecar (origin,
licence statement, SHA-256). Preferred: `neuromusic stimuli import --song <key> --file <path>`
with files from the rights holder. `neuromusic stimuli fetch --confirm-rights` downloads the
referenced videos' audio with the optional `yt-dlp` extra; the operator is responsible for the
platform's terms of service, which are separate from the copyright licence. Audio is never
committed. `neuromusic stimuli scan` renames files dropped into `data/stimuli/incoming/` (full
video titles are matched to songs; the original name and match score are kept in the sidecar).

## Playback alignment and coverage

The owner stated on 2026-10-29 that each EEG recording starts at the beginning of its song.
All non-synthetic recordings therefore have `playback_onset_s = 0` and
`alignment_verified = true`, with the statement stored as `alignment_evidence`
(`neuromusic align --onset 0 --evidence ...`). Onset precision (device start latency) is not
documented.

Every recording is shorter than its song (songs 157–440 s; recordings mostly ~121 s, some
21–94 s), so each recording covers only the opening portion of the song, and the covered
musical content differs between songs and between shorter and longer recordings of the same
song.
